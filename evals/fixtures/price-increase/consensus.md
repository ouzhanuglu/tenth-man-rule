# Consensus memo — 40% list price increase

**Decision:** raise list prices 40% across all segments effective Q1, no grandfathering.

**Confidence:** high (~90%).

**Evidence:**
1. NPS is 62, best in the category.
2. Annual churn is 1.2%.
3. CFO: competitors list about 15% above us, so we are underpriced.

**Assessment:** demand is inelastic at current price points. The increase lands at the top of the market, not above it.
